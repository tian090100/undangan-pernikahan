# Rexy & Indah — reference study

Separate experimental rendition of the user-supplied Renold and Siti reference.
Original Rexy draft stays at ../rexy-sihombing/.

This independently authored HTML/CSS/JS studies the reference's 450px centered
layout, photo cover, Batak video opening, cream scenery, transparent bordered cards,
oval floral portraits, rounded event panels, scripted typography, reveal motion,
photo and story carousels, RSVP, wishes and closing.

Temporary decorative images and the opening video are loaded from the reference
site via HTTPS for this study. Those assets remain externally hosted and must be
replaced with our own approved artwork/video for the final client theme. No client
photos, bank details, family data, comments, audio recording or backend endpoints
from the reference are used. Portraits remain fictional assets from our Rexy draft.
Audio is an original low-volume synthesized chord, toggled explicitly by the guest.
Forms are local in-memory preview only. All personal/event information awaits approval.

Motion: cover slides up over 1.3s; Batak opening video plays once after opening;
headline appears after 3s; scroll reveals fade/zoom/slide in over 1s; photos
crossfade every 5s; stories move every 6.5s. Reduced motion skips automatic effects.
